Week of November 2–8, 2026

Marketing

  • From 6 November our Shorts use voice-over: Messy moves through changing places while her voice tells the story, which gives more variety and removes visible lip-sync errors.
  • Clip checks now fail a Short only for flaws a viewer can actually see, and every Short cuts straight to the end card after the last word, with no dead seconds.
  • Daily cards now come from one shared renderer and pass an independent reviewer before approval, which ends layout drift and catches errors before posting.
  • Reach is now measured one way in a single Monday report, and no content test counts until each side has at least four posts, so one lucky post decides nothing.

Business & Operations

  • The 7 November Fund Update now links every token to its contract and lists each micro fund with its sessions and council, so readers can check everything.
  • Trading bots can find our Token Checker in public x402 directories: for 10 cents per query, it shows whether a Base, Ethereum or Robinhood Chain token is risky before buying.
  • We bought each of our seven x402 data products with a real payment, and every answer held all the fields its free sample promises, so buyers get what is advertised.
  • A bot that sends a wrong request or a rejected payment now gets a clear error instead of a server failure, so it can fix the call and retry.

Founders' project: Fund OS

  • Fund OS can now trade tokens that charge a transfer tax through a second route, so these tokens no longer depend on a single path to trade.
  • When one leg of a trade cannot find enough liquidity, Fund OS now drops it at once instead of spending retry rounds on it.
  • All seven micro funds held six council sessions each this week, and every session is listed in the public Fund Update for anyone to read.
