Week of November 16–22, 2026

Marketing

  • This week, our daily sector post on X became a short video card that compares each sector's 15-day move with the whole altcoin market, so readers see which sectors hold up.
  • For our next Shorts, we wrote two opening hooks per clip, an independent reviewer checked them, and we chose the stronger, so each clip opens with a sentence worth staying for.
  • When a Short fails its quality check, a reserve clip now takes its evening slot, so a new Short went out on YouTube and TikTok every evening this week.

Business & Operations

  • We published a card that explains the five rules our Token Checker uses to score a token's on-chain security from 0 to 100, so buyers know how to read each score.
  • The 21 November Fund Update now links a token only when its ticker appears in capitals, so words like cost stay plain text and each token link opens the right contract.
  • Our research agents now share one method: original sources first, a link on every key claim, and each link reopened before use, so our facts rest on pages we actually read.

Founders' project: Fund OS

  • Across seven micro funds, the founders' Fund OS councils met 35 times this week: 24 sessions executed a rebalance and 11 held, with each fund's count in the 21 November Fund Update.
