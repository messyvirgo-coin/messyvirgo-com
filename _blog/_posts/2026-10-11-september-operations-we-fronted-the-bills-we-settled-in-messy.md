The [August](/blog/2026/09/august-operations-we-fronted-the-bills-we-settled-in-messy/) pattern held. Most projects pay the stack by selling their token into the same pool you use. We do not.

September’s vendors were paid in **dollars**. After that, we settled between ourselves and the marketing wallet in **MESSY already sitting in treasury**, at the market rate on 11 October, **0.0005057 USD** per MESSY. We could have taken the reimbursement in USDC. We took most of it in the token you hold.

That is not a buy on the pool. It is not a DCA. It is not the 4.95% transfer tax paying Cursor. It is founders adding **MESSY exposure** after the invoices are already gone.

## What September looked like

September infrastructure (AI agents, data, core tooling) was **1,632.35 USD**.

Before any on-chain settlement:

- Michael had already paid **1,315.71 USD** (CHF 1,091.60) on his personal card.
- Franco had already paid **316.64 USD** from his personal card/wallet.

Then the books:

- Franco sent **499.54 USDC** to the marketing wallet (…9090fa), his half of the bill minus the 316.64 he had already paid.
- The marketing wallet sent **499.54 USDC** to Michael (…edfa40).
- The marketing wallet sent **1,614,000 MESSY** to Franco (…8a895a), valued at **816.18 USD** on 11 October.
- The marketing wallet sent **1,614,000 MESSY** to Michael (…edfa40), also valued at **816.18 USD**, because he had fronted **1,315.71 USD** of the month.

Vendors got fiat. The DAO did not sell MESSY into external liquidity. Both co-founders finished September with **more MESSY** than they started with, because settlement came out of treasury inventory and stayed on our wallets.

## Why that is the point

- **No stealth sell pressure.** Paying the stack did not mean dumping into your pool. When tokens moved, they moved from a known wallet, at a stated price.
- **We bleed in dollars first.** If MESSY went to zero tomorrow, the invoices are still paid. The reimbursement we took in token goes with it.
- **Conviction is a choice of currency.** The honest alternative was to take the whole month back in USDC. We didn’t.

If you hold MESSY, you are not backing a team that farms the book and exits into you. You are watching two people spend their own dollars on the machine, then keep more of the same token rather than cashing the month out.

The bills will keep coming. So will this settlement: in public, with the numbers, and without calling a treasury transfer a market buy.
